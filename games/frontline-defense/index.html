FIELD GUIDE · SECTOR 07

How to play

  1. Protect the base.Infantry that reaches the forward operating base removes one integrity. Hold through both Campaign waves, or survive as long as possible in Endless.
  2. Earn and spend field credits.Start with 320 credits. Destroyed enemies and completed waves award more; select a tower only when you can afford it.
  3. Place on clear terrain.Choose an emplacement, then click or tap a clear spot away from roads, scenery, other towers, and the map edge. Invalid positions never charge you.
  4. Let the defenses engage.Every tower automatically targets the furthest-along enemy in range. Inspect a placed tower for its stats; select another emplacement to continue building.
  5. Command the camera.Drag or use WASD / arrow keys to pan; use the wheel or zoom controls to zoom. Pause freezes enemies, towers, and projectiles.

Anti-Air emplacements are placeable now and can engage ground infantry. Flying enemies are not included in this build.
